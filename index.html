Rules of isometric drawing
  • Vertical edges stay vertical.
  • Length and width edges slope at 30°. There are no horizontal lines.
  • Each edge of a centimetre cube is one dot-gap long, so lengths are true.
  • Only draw the edges you can see.
  • You can outline every cube or just the edges of the solid. Both are marked correct.
  • You get 3 checks per question: 3 points first time, 2 second time, 1 third time. The answer appears after 3 tries.
  • Drag from dot to dot, or tap one dot then another. Keep tapping to draw a chain; tap the same dot to stop.
